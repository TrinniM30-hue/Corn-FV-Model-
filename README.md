# Corn-FV-Model-
Corn FV model based on stocks to use ratio.

Stocks to Use Ratio: Stocks to Use ratio is a key metric for agricultural commodity traders to assess the carryover stock (ending stock) as a percentage of annual total consumption. It shows how many days of supply is left before market runs out of supply, it also signifies price security, for eg:

             Low Ratio: Signifies that ending stocks are dangerously low compared to demand. If a weather shock or export spike occurs, there is no cushion. This typically triggers                          surging prices and high market volatility.
             High Ratio:  Signifies an abundance of grain or product. Buyers feel no urgency, resulting in depressed or stagnant prices as storage costs weigh on the market.
             Stocks to use ratio = (Ending Stocks / Total Use) × 100


Model Summary: 
Chart 1: It shows percent deviations from the model FV every year. From the final result, we can categorize the results in 4 distinct regimes. 
         1. persistent undervaluation 2000–2005 (pre-ethanol era)
         2. persistent overvaluation 2007–2013 (commodities super cycle and drought)
         3. growing overvaluation 2018–2022 (peak +30% in 2022)
         4. 2023–2024 close to fair, 2025 back to +17%.

Chart 2: It shows the inverse relationship between Stocks to Use ratio and prices via scatterplots with a fitted curve. The current 2025/26 observation is overvalued by 17%.
